# Build review and updated matrices — 0.19.0

Reviewed on 2026-10-26. Three new plug-in workflows extend the linear algebra
batch: **Linear combinations**, **Bases and coordinates**, and **Matrix products
and order**. The index adds their vocabulary and authored Related definitions
links. Content, numerical models, stories, views, vocabulary and map placement
retain separate contracts.

The collection has **29 draft cards, 149 worked examples, 238 numeric fixtures
and 37 canonical sources**. The 190-row coverage inventory contains 29 draft
specimens and 161 planned concepts. The written projection has 178 deterministic
BOH documents. There are 61 views: 29 Geometry, 29 Graph and the three existing
genuine 3D views. Eight territories and 24 map areas remain; Linear algebra now
has nine available lessons, with 75 distinct planned concepts mapped across the
whole landscape. The growing index has **213 entries: 194 authored definitions
and 19 contextual notation entries**, with 20 new authored definitions in this
release and 35 curated Related definitions groups. Index entries have their own
source links and preserve exact card notation.

## What works and what remains limited

| Area | Working in this build | Remaining or limited |
|---|---|---|
| Coherent expansion | Three connected workflows add 15 original worked examples and 36 canonical numeric fixtures | Every card remains draft; independent mathematical review and historical research remain open |
| Shared playground | New providers declare controls, evaluation, readouts and Geometry/Graph views; the existing host manages input, motion, playback, pins and lifecycle | A registered provider still needs explicit content, source evidence, quantity contracts and integration; no unrestricted expression editor is supplied |
| Linear combinations | Two real plane arrows are scaled by signed weights and added head to tail; both output components and the resulting length follow the same state | The live model has two vectors and bounded weights; the formal span uses all real coefficients and extends beyond the displayed box |
| Bases and coordinates | Keep a target fixed while changing two candidate directions; independent directions yield unique coefficients and reconstruction | A dependent pair is not a plane basis. A reachable target has infinitely many coefficient pairs; an unreachable target has no solution |
| Missing unique outputs | Basis configurations remain valid even when the primary output is null; singular examples are not displayed as zero coordinates | Near-dependence can require large weights; numeric-range failure is explicitly different from dependence or no solution |
| Matrix product order | Input, intermediate and final points connect two edits; the opposite-order endpoint and selected product show why AB and BA can differ | Live matrices are only one horizontal and one vertical real 2D shear. The order choice is discrete; no arbitrary matrix product calculator is claimed |
| Mathematical distinctions | AB applies B first for column vectors; both shears preserve signed area; matching endpoints at zero do not prove equal maps | Area preservation does not imply length/angle preservation. A single animated input cannot establish equality of transformations |
| Representations | All 29 modules have Geometry and Graph; composition, logarithm and derivative retain their genuine 3D views | New modules add no 3D. Graphs are declared scalar slices, while scenes/readouts retain both vector coordinates |
| Story and number pacing | New four-step stories use the shared input → calculation → shape trace, linked quantity marks and existing readable-number cadence | Stories use stipulated values rather than measured systems; learner comprehension and physical-device readability still need direct qualification |
| Definitions and search | New vocabulary links back to cards; Related definitions links support paths through neighboring meanings; shared and focused search remain available | Related links are editorial navigation, not sourced theorems or mandatory preparation. The index is not a complete mathematics dictionary |
| Context-sensitive notation | Local uses retain their meanings: a matrix B differs from an event B, while basis coefficients r/s differ from other cards' uses | Matching letters or words does not establish matching quantities, units or experimental meaning |
| Atlas | Existing eight territories and 24 areas retained; new cards join Linear algebra and their explained concept neighborhoods | Only 75 distinct planned concepts are projected into the landscape; this is not all 161 planned inventory entries. Relation-level filtering and a multi-concept matrix remain |
| Private state | Existing notebook, workspace and explicit quantity-connection mechanisms remain separate from canonical content and index metadata | Fresh-profile recovery and broader device/assistive-technology qualification remain open; no automatic wiring or implicit conversion |
| Verification | Actual schema/reference/AST checks, all 238 fixtures and deterministic 178-document export pass; the new provider matches all 36 canonical cases, with focused model tests covering basis solution classes | Final release-wide totals and any actual browser evidence belong in the release verification record. Tests do not establish independent mathematical review, historical accuracy or teaching effectiveness |

## Principles to add or enforce next

| Priority | Principle | Status | Concrete next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plug-in workflows keep their own mathematics | Implemented baseline | Continue separate providers for models, stories, rendering and content; extend declared host contracts without topic-specific host branches |
| 1 | Configuration validity differs from answer existence | Enforced for basis cases | Preserve valid configurations with no unique result; distinguish unique, infinite-solutions, no-solution and numeric-range states in future inverse problems |
| 1 | A general concept can have a small honest model | Explicit in the new cards | Keep the full definition separate from two-vector, two-dimensional, bounded-control and two-shear restrictions |
| 1 | Operation order is part of the expression | Implemented with separate product ASTs | Keep the order enum, intermediate point, selected product and final calculation synchronized; do not interpolate a discrete operation order |
| 1 | A numerical range limit is not a theorem | Model distinction implemented; qualification open | Keep clipping and unavailable finite results visible; test near-dependence without silently treating a small nonzero determinant as zero |
| 1 | Vocabulary and content expand together | Implemented baseline; ongoing gate | Include definitions, examples, exact local notation and card links with each new batch; explain new technical words where they first appear |
| 2 | Related meanings need explicit link intent | Related navigation implemented; editorial gate ongoing | Keep vocabulary exploration distinct from prerequisites, proof, equivalence and empirical analogy; audit ambiguous shared letters |
| 2 | A graph states which quantity it plots | Implemented baseline | Preserve combined horizontal output, first basis coefficient and final horizontal output as named scalar slices; retain their other coordinates in scene/text |
| 2 | Continuous motion uses readable values | Shared mechanism retained; qualification open | Qualify linked traces, paced labels and near-dependent large coefficients with actual learners and access methods |
| 2 | One matching input is weak evidence | Explicit in the product examples | Keep counterexamples and conditions close to claims; do not infer equal transformations or commutativity from origin agreement |
| Next | Relations can be compared across concepts | Planned under T14 | Add relation-level filters and the multi-concept matrix while retaining conditions and evidence |
| Next | Breadth requires review and direct learning evidence | Review and qualification open | Track draft counts separately from mathematical/history review, and record focused learner, accessibility and recovery results |
