# Build review and updated matrices — 0.22.0

Reviewed on 2026-10-26. This batch prioritizes actual gaps in the atlas:
Sets and membership, Population and sample, and Computational complexity: a
linear scan make the formerly empty Sets and proof, Statistics and Linear-time
algorithms areas playable. All 24 areas now contain at least one available
lesson. This is minimum introductory availability, not completion of a field.

The collection contains **38 draft cards, 194 original worked examples, 346
numeric fixtures, 47 canonical sources and 232 deterministic BOH documents**.
The 192-row inventory contains 38 draft specimens and 154 planned concepts.
There are 83 views: 38 Geometry, 38 Graph and seven genuine Spatial views.
Eight territories and 24 areas retain 70 distinct mapped planned concepts.
The index currently has 409 entries: 389 authored definitions and 20 contextual
readers. Actual final checks and browser observations are recorded in
[release verification](VALIDATION_0_22_0.md).

The [sealed baseline](NOTATION_GATE_BEFORE_EXPANSION.json),
[0.20.0 checkpoint](NOTATION_COVERAGE_0_20_0.json) and
[0.21.0 checkpoint](NOTATION_COVERAGE_0_21_0.json) remain preserved. New forms
enter the separate [current report](NOTATION_COVERAGE_0_22_0.json) under the
same finite [notation contract](SYMBOL_NOTATION_COVERAGE.md). An older passed
gate does not establish coverage of a later card, story, unit or diagram label.
The expanded current report checks 23,794 fields and resolves 39,023 occurrences
across 916 forms, with zero unresolved forms and zero callable reviews.

## What works and what remains limited

| Area | Working in this build | Limitation or remaining qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas priority | The three previously empty areas now each have an introductory playable lesson; all 24 areas have actual availability | Sets and proof still needs proof methods, Statistics still needs inference and further summaries, and linear-time methods are only one part of complexity |
| Sets and membership | Inclusive moving cutoffs select distinct labeled pegs. The result remains a set; cardinality is a separate integer count. Intersection, union and ordered difference keep their own meanings | Fixed twelve-candidate universe only; not interval measure, arbitrary set parsing, multiset algebra or a proof checker |
| Exact boundaries | Equality can retain a singleton when the two limits coincide. Smooth cutoff motion changes the count only at actual member boundaries | A visual overlap or zero cutoff gap does not substitute for membership predicates or cardinality |
| Population and sample | All twelve crate observations remain visible alongside selected labels, separate sums, arithmetic means and signed mean difference | A deterministic cyclic window in a known finite population; no random sample, sampling distribution, confidence interval or unbiasedness guarantee |
| Observation identity | Equal crate weights remain distinct observations. A full census makes the two means agree; changing an excluded value leaves the selected mean fixed | A set of unique measured values would be a different object and must not silently replace labeled observations |
| Linear scan | The specified procedure inspects each item once. Numbered work tiles expose the chosen operation count, with T(n)=cn and a doubled-input comparison | Chosen basic operation and fixed stipulated per-item cost only; no measured benchmark, early-stop search, nested algorithm or arbitrary program analyzer |
| Growth classes | O(n) and Θ(n) stay distinct from numeric counts and modeled time. The full scan rule supplies an all-positive-integer formula | The finite display alone does not prove a complexity class. At c=0 modeled time is zero, while n inspections still occur |
| Discrete domains | Sample size, starting index and scan item count use whole-number states. Operation choices remain discrete; cutoff, weight and cost sweeps can be smooth | Visual transitions do not create fractional members, observations or work items |
| Story-to-scene links | Three workshop narratives use four steps, calculation phases and actual quantity marks to follow changed inputs through formulas into scenes | Stipulated original scenarios and data, not measured inventories, equipment results or performance promises |
| Shared modular host | Models, stories, canonical records, previews, index definitions and atlas placement remain separate. Existing motion, pacing, controls, pins and saved state are reused | New topics still need explicit controls, quantities, domains and reader coverage; no topic-specific animation engine is implied |
| Distinct representations | Every card has Geometry and Graph. The three gap lessons keep two views while the existing seven Spatial capabilities remain | Extra dimensions are added only for a distinct mathematical purpose, with usable text and data alternatives |
| Search and notation | New set, descriptive-statistics and algorithm-analysis readers have actual card-use links. Cardinality bars, mean bars, summation and bound classes retain context | The finite gate resolves all 39,023 occurrences in this corpus. Ambiguous spelling still needs continuing scope review; related definitions remain editorial navigation, not inferred canonical relationships |
| Units and quantity types | Count, index, sum, mean, time per item and total time remain distinct; ms/item multiplied by item count produces modeled ms | Similar names do not permit automatic wiring or implicit unit conversion |
| Canonical evidence | Fifteen new examples and 36 fixtures agree with the provider; content checks preserve set/scalar roles, selection assumptions and exact scan limits | Numerical agreement is not independent mathematical, historical or pedagogical review |
| Offline and written corpus | The three new allowlisted modules and records join the standalone application and deterministic export pipeline | Direct file/device qualification, broader recovery tests and live BOH ingestion/retrieval remain separate from static checks |

## Principles to add or enforce next

| Priority | Principle | Current status | Concrete next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Expansion follows actual coverage gaps | The three empty areas received the next lessons | Track both available introductions and deeper missing concepts; avoid treating one lesson as a completed field |
| 1 | One state feeds story, formula and scene | The new cards use evaluated quantities and semantic marks | Keep each new input, intermediate and output linked to an actual correctly typed mark |
| 1 | A collection differs from its cardinality | Set objects and numeric counts have separate bindings | Preserve object-versus-count roles through grammar, comparison and future set operations |
| 1 | Discrete membership can accompany continuous motion | Threshold bands move while fixed-member counts change at boundaries | Never interpolate a fractional member or use a drawing's overlap area as a count |
| 1 | Observation identity differs from measured value | Equal weights remain separate crate observations | Retain labels when selecting or aggregating repeated values |
| 1 | Selection method differs from sample size | The cyclic sample is explicitly deterministic at every size | Introduce random sampling and inference only with their own mechanisms and assumptions |
| 1 | A statistic differs from a population parameter | Sample and population sums, means and denominators remain separate | Preserve the collection being summarized and the declared sign of their difference |
| 1 | A chosen operation differs from whole runtime | One inspection per item is counted before assigning its modeled cost | Name the basic operation, excluded overhead and cost assumption with every new algorithm lesson |
| 1 | Asymptotic class differs from finite evidence | The procedure supplies w(n)=n beyond the bounded display | Do not infer a growth class from a few plotted or benchmarked points |
| 1 | Zero modeled cost does not erase work | c=0 retains n inspections while T(n)=0 | Keep zero boundaries and applicable lower-bound conditions explicit |
| 1 | A symbol gets meaning from context | Cardinality/absolute bars, mean/conjugate bars, ∑/Σ and O/Θ remain scoped | Require exact contextual aliases and actual ownership for future ambiguous forms |
| 2 | Units describe a relationship, not just a label | ms/item and item counts produce modeled ms; kilogram sums and means stay distinct from counts | Preserve declared units and reject implicit conversions or name-based wiring |
| 2 | New views earn their mathematical role | The gap lessons keep Geometry and Graph without decorative 3D | Add another representation only when it reveals a distinct relationship and can be explained accessibly |
| 2 | Every new word arrives with a usable route | New vocabulary has definitions and actual card links | Audit explanatory prose and table captions alongside formulas and symbols |
| Next | Learning and access require direct evidence | T12 qualification remains open | Observe learners reading boundaries, chosen samples and complexity claims across real devices and access needs |
| Next | Tests and draft counts do not replace review | Independent mathematics/history review remains open | Track review status and claim-level historical evidence separately from catalog breadth |
