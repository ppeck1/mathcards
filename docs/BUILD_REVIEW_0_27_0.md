# 0.27 book-guided build review

This first batch adds four gateways from the supplied book scopes. It does not
claim to implement the entire 214-topic matrix. The [roadmap](BOOK_CONCEPT_ROADMAP.md)
and [complete topic table](BOOK_CONCEPT_ROADMAP.csv) preserve that work and its
scope boundaries for later batches.

## Added concepts

| Atlas gap | Added lesson | Story, formula and visible change |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations: truth-functional reasoning | Logical connectives and truth tables | A panel length crosses an inclusive requirement; two test results select a connective's truth-table row and a region of the input plane |
| Foundations: every versus some | Universal and existential quantifiers | One minimum-length requirement changes six explicit predicate outcomes and the separate universal/existential claims |
| Structures and geometry: oriented vector products | Cross product and oriented area | Turn an edge of a hinged frame; follow its perpendicular component into signed normal and nonnegative area |
| Dynamics: equilibrium response | Stability of an equilibrium | Change one multiplier; compare exact signed correction errors with their analytic power rule and distance from zero |

## What works and what remains

| Principle | Current result | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Plug-and-play modules | One pure provider supplies four models/scenes; one story provider supplies narrative; canonical records remain separate | Keep subsequent families out of shared-host topic branches |
| Connected representations | Shared evaluated values drive Geometry, Graph, story tokens, previews, pins and index links | Broader arbitrary-vector and arbitrary-formula engines remain separate work |
| Simple guided interaction | One HTML range per compact story, with optional calculation details and advanced controls | Physical touch-device and learner review remains open |
| Readable semantics | True/False labels, semantic pin comparisons and live announcements; stable full-contrast text | Screen-reader qualification remains separate from automated host checks |
| Honest discrete behavior | Truth changes only at exact included thresholds; completed updates remain integers; graphs label categorical or discrete states | Finite pictures do not establish infinite-domain claims or general stability theorems |
| Reference coverage | 455 entries, 573 selectable forms and scoped predicate, vector and stability meanings | Add each future notation delta before publishing its lesson |
| Meaningful atlas | Foundations grows from 3 to 5 introductions, Structures 13 to 14, Spaces 5 to 6 and Change 14 to 15 | Counts describe available introductions, not field mastery; territories overlap |
| Research trail | Six new primary definition sources and the matrix's stable research IDs | Independent mathematics, edition-specific history and pedagogy remain unreviewed |
