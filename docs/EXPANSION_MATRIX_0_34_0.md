## Executable expansion

Every row below has canonical cards, original worked examples, model-derived
Geometry and Graph, a connected story, matching previews, reference entries,
actual card links and independent numeric references. Spatial remains reserved
for the seven existing lessons with genuine 3D representations.

| Family | New lessons | Mathematical boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Probability models | Binomial distribution; Poisson distribution | Independent Bernoulli trials or a homogeneous Poisson count model; finite displayed windows retain the omitted tail |
| Sampling and inference | Sampling distribution; confidence interval coverage; hypothesis test | Exhaustive small sampling, fixed-size exact binomial intervals and a declared one-sided binomial test |
| Statistical modeling | Linear regression; correlation; factorial interaction; chi-square association | Constructed datasets, explicit residuals and assumptions; no causal or clinical claim |
| Information | Relative entropy | Directed finite-distribution comparison; support mismatch is infinite, not zero |
| Continuous dynamics | Vector fields; continuous flows; basins of attraction | Stated linear field and exactly solved scalar families; finite-time position stays distinct from a limiting destination |
| Equilibrium changes | Fold bifurcation; cusp potential | Local normal forms and stationary points; approximate root positions do not imply a history-dependent trajectory |
| Order structures | Partial orders; meet and join; distributive lattices | All subsets of three named tools; subset membership differs from internal collection encoding; not every lattice is distributive |
| Physical models | Ideal gas; heat exchange and physical entropy; Gibbs free energy | Explicit units, fixed heat capacities and stated equilibrium assumptions; favorability does not give reaction speed |
| Neural abstractions | Artificial neuron; Hebbian update | One sigmoid unit and one local correlation update; no anatomical or complete memory model |

The near-term sampling, relative-entropy and dynamics queue now has executable
introductions. The entire mathematical roadmap is larger than this queue.
Hysteresis, general manifolds, structural stability, arbitrary proof editing,
general tensor laws and many other advanced topics remain planned. A cusp
potential is not labeled as a completed hysteresis simulator.
