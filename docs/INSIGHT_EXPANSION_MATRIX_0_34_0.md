Continuation of [draft PR #13](https://github.com/ppeck1/mathcards/pull/13),
2026-11-09–10. The authorized expansion scope is now the **entire eight-book
roadmap**, rather than only the current number/geometry sequence. This first

The live site remains 0.33.0.

## Connected lessons

| Lesson | Story → formula → measured visual | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Irrational square root | Brace a square display frame; follow its side into its diagonal and compare rational estimates | Eight finite rational convergents approximate √2; a separate parity proof establishes irrationality |
| Golden ratio | Cut a square from a panel and compare the remaining rectangle's proportions | The positive solution of φ²=φ+1 differs from every displayed finite Fibonacci ratio; no aesthetics claim |
| Circle measurement | Choose a round tabletop radius; follow it into edge band, diameter and covering area | π is circumference divided by diameter; circumference and disk area have different units |
| Floor and ceiling | Store an ideal liquid batch; count full containers and containers needed | Whole counts change at exact input thresholds; no additional container at an exact fit |
| Triangle measurement | Shift a triangular awning's apex while choosing base and perpendicular height | Area is bh/2 even when the height foot is outside the base; perimeter can change under shear |
| Similar triangles | Enlarge a 3–4–5 support template | Corresponding lengths scale by k and area by k² on one fixed drawing scale |
| Perpendicular bisector | Swing equal compass circles from two marked points | Two distinct intersections define the bisector; one tangent point does not define that construction |
| Ellipse | Lay out an elliptical garden with a fixed-length string between two foci | Two focal distances sum to 2a; the circle case and numerical point parameter remain explicit |
| Platonic solids | Choose a regular-solid display frame and count its structure | Five discrete convex regular solids; projected edges illustrate connectivity, not volume |
| Tessellation | Fit regular tiles around one floor junction | Signed angle gaps differ from overlaps; actual triangle/square/hexagon patterns accompany compatible cases |
| Perspective projection | Size an ideal camera image by moving a flat object | Similar triangles give image width fW/z; a fixed coordinate scale preserves depth effects |
| Sphere packing | Compare simple-cubic and face-centered-cubic storage cells | Shared sphere contributions and contact distances determine unit-cell fractions; no global densest-packing proof |

Three explicit providers reuse the shared host. Stories, model evaluation,
previews, build-only reference entries and canonical cards remain separate.
Controls, clocks, paced numbers, private-state schemas and independent comparison
slots retain their existing owners. Discrete counts and selectors do not invent
fractional intermediate states.

## Entire roadmap

The [generated progress matrix](ROADMAP_PROGRESS.md) covers all eighteen subject
areas and lists every remaining planned subject once. The
[full subject matrix](EIGHT_BOOK_SUBJECT_MATRIX.csv) preserves all 295 stable
topic IDs, exact card links, scope boundaries and supplied book evidence states.
The eight-book inventory records 96 bounded introductions, 55 precursors and
144 planned subjects. These overlapping inventories are not counts of new
lessons.

After these geometric prerequisites, expansion continues through mathematical
language, algebra, analysis, discrete reasoning and proof, order/lattice theory,
statistics/design, information/computation, neural models, thermodynamics and
physics, with history and philosophy supported by original sources. Closely
related topics can share a lesson when its scope actually introduces each one.
Numeric examples alone do not establish general theorems or historical claims.
