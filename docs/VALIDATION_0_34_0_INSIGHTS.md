## Current scope

The candidate has 142 draft lessons, 714 original examples, 2,747 independent
numeric fixtures, 856 written exports, 291 declared views and 142 native MathML
definitions. Seventeen explicit metadata families install 58 continuation
modules through the existing host. The branch adds 81 lessons to the published
61-card baseline. Existing model revisions and private-state schemas retain
their owners.

The twelve lessons add square diagonals and rational convergents, golden
rectangles, circular measurement, floor/ceiling container counts, triangle
area under shear, similarity, compass bisectors, focal ellipses, Platonic
solids, regular tilings, pinhole perspective and periodic sphere packing.
Their domains and limits are in the [current matrix](INSIGHT_EXPANSION_MATRIX_0_34_0.md)
and canonical cards. No finite demonstration substitutes for a general theorem.

The parity contradiction establishes that √2 is irrational; finite convergents
approximate that ratio. Arbitrary scaled lengths are not all irrational.
Container counts use exact entered decimal thresholds, with remaining liquid
and unused capacity kept consistent even immediately above a threshold.
Triangle area retains its perpendicular-height meaning when its height foot
is outside the base. A tangent compass pair has one intersection, so the
two-point construction is not available there. Near-tangent intersections
retain nonzero separation rather than lose it through squared subtraction.

Solid skeletons have unit-length edges and verified incidence counts.
Their projections encode connectivity rather than volume. Regular tile angle
compatibility is distinguished from a global tiling proof; the compatible
triangle, square and hexagon cases show actual patches. Pinhole dimensions
follow ray-plane intersections. Sphere packing uses periodic fractional
boundary ownership and correct contact distances; a planar face illustration
is distinct from the separately measured volume fraction.

Definition-level sources and inspected locators are recorded in the three
family integration files and [source catalog](../content/sources.json).
Scenarios, worked calculations and diagrams are original constructions.
Book associations remain attributed planning evidence, with exact-edition and
page checks and independent expert/teaching review still open.
